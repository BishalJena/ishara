# Ishara hackathon demo feasibility

## 1. Which end-to-end direction can become a compelling real demonstration by submission?

### Takeaway

The strongest differentiation is a person completing an ordinary task through signing, retaining control when the hearing counterpart changes the plan. Preserve the native-call ambition as a time-boxed candidate, but treat meaningful Bolna as mandatory and allow an explicit pivot: supervised Bolna task calls are the most direct fit to its documented hosted strengths; in-person communication removes the cellular audio barrier; app-owned calling offers control at the price of a new calling experience. None has passed a runtime gate.

### Cited Findings

- The official buildathon page gives October 31 submission, November 3 shortlist/interview invitations, November 5–7 interviews, November 8 finalist results, and November 20 finale. It describes building a real voice project, top ten tickets, and top three stage pitches. It does not provide scoring weights in the inspected body; “impact” is a design hypothesis, not a sourced weighting. The task's mandatory Bolna requirement comes from the founder and should be accepted as controlling. [Official buildathon](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)
- There are roughly thirteen calendar days from research date October 18 to October 31; a good initial submission must stand on its own before the later finale, rather than putting every unresolved core dependency off until November. This timing is arithmetic based on the event dates, not organizer advice. [Official timeline](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)
- Ordinary-call app speech has an Apple-supported mechanism, whereas the combination of camera, injection, captions, device routes and installed OS still requires execution. Another app does not acquire the unrelated native call's remote audio. Those constraints make the native route distinctive but leave Ishara without an app-owned incoming transcript. [Apple synthesized speech into calls](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/adding-synthesized-speech-to-calls); [Apple DTS on unrelated call audio](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/841340)
- Bolna graph event injection lets a backend change a live agent's node and supply properties. Events are queued during active speech/generation; if the customer is speaking when an event resolves, proactive speech can be skipped. A 202 acknowledgment is event publication, not delivery or faithful utterance evidence. [Event injection](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/graph-agent/event-injection.md)
- Bolna static messages are synthesized on agent save. Their cited approximately 50 ms cached playback is useful for repeated greetings/hold messages, and cannot be extrapolated to arbitrary fresh signing. Silence replay is configurable, meaning a signing pause should not be interpreted automatically as abandonment. [Static nodes and silence repeat](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/graph-agent/static-nodes.md)
- An Indian DHH study with 15 interviews and 131 survey responses, focusing on employed participants, documents reliance on others for ride/food-service communication and service-provider calls for directions/availability. It also discusses agency/privacy and linguistic diversity. This supports investigating pickup coordination as an everyday problem, without claiming a representative sample of every Deaf Indian or current service failure rates. [Primary study](https://roshni-poddar.github.io/assets/pdf/interact23a-sub1087-cam-i7.pdf)
- The Bolna builders page already showcases diverse voice assistants, including hiring, gig-worker matching and elder-care check-ins. Merely wrapping a standard agent is weak differentiation; this is an inference about positioning, not a competitor count or judging rule. [Bolna builders](https://www.bolna.ai/builders)

### Inferences

**Feasibility matrix — relative engineering judgment, not executed results or a probability of winning.** The OSS text/audio adapter described by the coordinator's Bolna research is a real engineering branch to consider; it is not a documented hosted standalone TTS endpoint. Its inspected input-routing issue must be fixed or bypassed explicitly before relying on it.

| Direction | Everyday experience and core Bolna job | Why it could stand out | Principal deadline risk | One or two decisive tests; stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existing Phone call + AAC | User makes/answers ordinary call, opens Ishara, signs; Bolna OSS adapter accepts committed semantics and produces speech audio; Apple injects it. User reads system captions. | Closest to the founder's seamless existing-number ambition; counterpart installs nothing. | Unsupported hosted text-to-audio path; OSS adapter/audio work; iOS concurrency; no app-readable return transcript; caption locale. | (1) Known exact text through actual Bolna audio pipeline and AAC reaches consenting receiver during camera capture/captions. (2) Sign-derived second turn works after a long pause without unwanted speech. The window is a proposed prioritization rule. |
| Face-to-face at a counter | User opens Ishara while already talking to receptionist/shopkeeper; phone camera captures signer, phone mic captures hearing speech, Bolna orchestrates ASR/faithful speech through an explicit multimodal adapter. | Strong demonstration of two-way communication and actual counterpart context; no second call. | Input channels/turn policy, TTS-to-ASR echo, mic and camera composition, noise; OSS/hosted session interface still needs qualification. | (1) Alternate externally supplied committed text and live hearing speech in one Bolna session, display actual ASR and play exact intended output with no self-echo loop. (2) Counterpart changes one option; user corrects; app remains silent during signing pause. (2) User rejects/changes proposal through signing, event is honored exactly once, final agreement is grounded in counterpart response. If no timely verified return channel/approval loop exists, do not claim live supervised calling; fall back to explicitly preauthorized narrow constraints or reject this variant. |
| App-owned assisted call | A single “Call with Ishara” flow owns signaling/media and exposes actual incoming speech; signs enter Bolna-supported orchestration; recipient should still use a normal telephone where carrier/PSTN integration permits. | Coherent in-app captions, speech, interruption state and consent; stronger product surface than sidecar. | Telecom setup, identity/caller ID, call routing/SDK, two media legs, ASR events, echo, app delivery. This is a deviation from native Phone. | (1) Actual phone recipient hears outgoing sign-derived speech and their reply reaches app-owned text in one session. (2) User interrupts/corrects without duplicate/stale audio or invented acceptance. If making the recipient install an app or requiring hidden operator routing is necessary, disclose it and reconsider seamlessness. |

**Recommended direction hierarchy:** pursue a genuine two-way sign-mediated task with Bolna essential, not “25 signs plus a voice API.” Use a short physical/adapter gate to preserve the native-call concept if it works. If it fails, select the deviation that has the fewest remaining unverified dependencies, rather than promising all four modes. A hosted supervised task call fits Bolna's established agent-call product better than pretending it is a commodity TTS endpoint; an in-person adapter preserves direct conversation and removes OS access barriers, but still requires nontrivial Bolna transport work. The final choice must incorporate the coordinator's current code/API audit, not just the prior bounded not-found result.

**What would justify the ranking:** native Phone should rank first if an actual Bolna OSS text-to-audio adapter can produce faithful output alongside camera/captions on the target phone, and the user's return language works; it then preserves the preferred experience and avoids building a separate live-ASR supervision UI. Supervised delegation should rank first only if its actual call-to-app proposal channel and explicit approval loop are materially easier to make reliable than that adapter/iOS composition. The existence of hosted outbound calling alone does not establish those cross-channel features or imply lower total effort. Its contact/task activation and different call identity are a real workflow deviation. If both pass, favor native Phone for the founder's original seamlessness goal. If neither passes, in-person is a different architecture candidate, not an automatically safe fallback. Source research cannot choose a runtime winner.

**Strong task candidate:** pickup coordination, supported by the study's actual service-call friction. This can be narrower than appointment booking: the user's saved pickup card provides an exact approved location, and a deliberately recognized intent activates it. Driver asks for a change; user reads the actual reply and deliberately refuses or chooses another approved location. Never expand a generic LOCATION sign into an unsupplied gate number. On the native route the app cannot see the driver's proposed location; only the user can read it in system captions. A supervised agent route can use actual received ASR but must expose the proposal before acceptance.

**Alternative:** a clinic appointment remains a legible stage narrative, especially for a supervised agent. Keep it strictly administrative: request, availability, propose, reject, approve. Do not use symptom diagnosis or emergency decisions to manufacture emotional stakes. Neither scenario's signs are covered automatically by a generic 25-label model.

### Gaps

- No scoring rubric, exact deadline timezone, submission-video length, mandatory artifact list or “OSS versus hosted integration qualifies” rule was established. This does not suspend product research or make mandatory Bolna an open question.
- No device session, event call, ASR channel, speech-generation request or inference was executed. There is no demonstrated complete variant.
- The team has not validated pickup needs, phone positioning, English reading or confirmation preferences with a Deaf participant. The cited study supports a research hypothesis, not Ishara usability.

## 2. What recognition and turn capture are actually credible within that scope?

### Takeaway

Use a bounded temporal-recognition baseline or a bounded phrase comparator, and select the exact vocabulary from one complete task. Current public resources provide real pretrained isolated-sign starting points; they do not supply a verified low-latency continuous ISL translator. Jev images deserve a short comparison, not an assumption that generic vision plus confidence solves signing.

### Cited Findings

- AI4Bharat's INCLUDE repository documents pretrained inference and exposes `evaluate.py`. The inspected evaluator constructs a 263-class transformer, loads an exact label map and pretrained weights, extracts hands/pose features and returns the highest class. This is file-based isolated classification without an unknown gate or natural continuous endpoint. It also pads to a fixed frame length, so arbitrary-length capture requires engineering rather than feeding the stream unmodified. [Repository](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/INCLUDE); [evaluator source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AI4Bharat/INCLUDE/master/evaluate.py)
- The inspected pretrained-link manifest contains concrete non-CNN LSTM/transformer weight URLs and literal `link` placeholders for CNN entries. Source presence is not proof that the weight URLs are presently downloadable or compatible with today's environment; no model binary was fetched. [Weight manifest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AI4Bharat/INCLUDE/master/pretrained_links.json)
- OpenHands lists INCLUDE pretrained BiLSTM, BERT, SL-GCN and ST-GCN checkpoints with metadata/configuration, and documents inference on supplied video/pose files. Its repository explicitly says it is no longer actively maintained. It is a serious pretrained baseline route with runtime/dependency risk, not a fresh supported iPhone SDK. [Checkpoint table](https://openhands.ai4bharat.org/en/latest/instructions/models.html); [inference documentation](https://openhands.ai4bharat.org/en/latest/instructions/inference.html); [maintenance notice](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/OpenHands)
- The inspected 263-class INCLUDE map includes `location`, `door`, `streetorroad`, `office`, `house`, `car`, `minute`, `time`, `today`, `tomorrow`, `hello`, `thankyou` and colors. It does **not** contain `wait`, `repeat`, `stop`, `left`, `right`, `yes`, `no`, `gate`, `appointment`, or number classes. `waiter` and `secondnumber` must not be substituted for WAIT or a general number recognizer. This is an exact label-map audit, not a video/variant-quality audit. [Class map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AI4Bharat/INCLUDE/master/label_maps/label_map_include.json)
- The public `islkit` repository offers temporal landmarks/features, a TCN and confidence-gated decline, but explicitly ships no pretrained weights. It warns that its convenient landmark dump has no signer/session metadata and optimistic splits; it is a reference architecture rather than an immediately working recognition engine. [Author repository](https://github.com/jbrathwa/islkit)
- iSign publishes video/pose-to-text tasks and more than 118k paired examples, with many source examples drawn from news/educational material. Its project page reports human checking only a 593-pair sample. This supports source-phrase auditing, not an assumption of appointment/pickup phrase coverage or gold labels for every sentence. [Project and validation](https://exploration-lab.github.io/iSign/); [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.643/)
- ISLTranslate has sentence/phrase video data and extracted pose archives; its public repository reports human checking of 291 pairs and noncommercial dataset terms. Its license paragraph mistakenly calls the dataset CISLR, so provenance/terms need qualification rather than silently granting commercial reuse. [ISLTranslate repository](https://github.com/Exploration-Lab/ISLTranslate)
- MediaPipe iOS Hand Landmarker supports asynchronous timestamped live-stream detection. It can ignore a new frame if still processing the prior frame. It detects hand geometry; there is no claim here that it understands ISL. Match the recognizer's exact feature convention and normalization rather than swap in a new detector and assume pretrained compatibility. [Official iOS guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker/ios); [INCLUDE evaluator geometry](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AI4Bharat/INCLUDE/master/evaluate.py)
- Jev's current primary guide accepts PNG/JPEG still images, assets inside state/criteria, visual examples for choice options and ordered frame histories with `TypeSafe-Danger-Zone-Images: true`. Still-frame histories are supported input; native video or ISL recognition accuracy is not established. Each image must meet resolution limits, repeated references count toward expanded limits and usage is billed as input tokens. [Jev image guide](https://docs.typesafe.ai/images)
- Jev confidence summarizes the option probability distribution; it is not ground-truth correctness. The provider explicitly calls for domain-specific threshold evaluation. Context7's fetched choice docs also recommend an other/none option. [Confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence); [Choice](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/choice)
- Sign languages use hands, arms, face and body; some Deaf people do not comfortably read caption text at speech speed. A hands-only detector and an assumed English return channel are both incomplete persona claims. [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/sign-languages/)

### Inferences

**Smallest honest recognition commitment:** a set of perhaps 8–12 task utterances or intents, with exact approved semantic meaning, including request, negative/correction, wait/repeat and finish. This is a proposed scope, not a sourced natural-language inventory. Audit real source clips, variant, reuse permission and held-out coverage before fixing the count. Single signs may activate explicitly approved saved cards; disclose that this is bounded intent recognition plus context, not literal translation of arbitrary ISL. If no natural clips/competent reviewer can cover the essential repair phrases, this is an actual schedule blocker, not something Bolna/Jev can infer away.

**Recognition experiment order:** (1) recover one genuine temporal pretrained baseline and exact feature/label mapping; evaluate the task's available signs without retraining an entire continuous translator; (2) compare a small Jev ordered-frame/reference-phrase trial, including unknown/idle/insufficient-view choices; (3) add contextual reranking only if it reduces committed semantic errors on held-out examples. Do not gate speech on the model's confidence alone. A laptop/cloud baseline can be acceptable for a disclosed connected hackathon prototype; do not claim on-device/offline because the camera is on iPhone.

**Turn capture options:**

| Option | Benefit | Failure mode and disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| Rolling temporal window + detected signing boundaries | Closest to ordinary signing; no tap per utterance | Resting hands can occur inside an utterance; overlapping windows can repeat the same speech; face/body and intentional end need evaluation. |
| Bounded whole-phrase retrieval against reviewed naturally signed examples | Avoids pretending English-order word concatenation is natural ISL | Needs actual task-specific phrase examples and robustness to variant/signers; can become memorized gesture shortcuts. |
| A visible hold-to-sign or tap-to-send fallback | Makes capture and irreversible speech commitment explicit | Imposes a new step and can occupy a signing hand. Label it as a fallback; do not call it seamless natural conversation. |
| Always-on nod/shake YES/NO | Superficially fewer taps | Listening motion and linguistic nonmanuals can be mistaken for permission; not acceptable as ungated consent. |

**Critical falsifiable test:** give each candidate a blinded supported phrase set plus natural idle motion, pauses, unsupported signing, partial framing and a self-correction. Record semantic output and silence decisions. A new recording session is more useful than replaying training clips; a different competent signer is better where obtainable. If the candidate cannot distinguish intended negative/correction from idle or produces unsupported speech, reduce automatic commitment or reject that recognizer. Do not conceal taps, a human operator or canned triggering during the demo.

**Jev stopping rule:** keep it only if it improves the same held-out task cohort over the specialist baseline at acceptable end-to-end latency. Ordered stills may lose movement that distinguishes signs; a confident wrong choice is worse than a visible request to re-sign. No requirement exists to use Jev, Laya or a generative model just because they are available.

### Gaps

- No pretrained binary, corpus clip, checkpoint usability, target-device performance, phrase coverage or cloud-image inference was tested. A listed URL is a candidate to test, not “working out of the box.”
- This audit checked INCLUDE's exact label map, not every CISLR/iSign/other corpus label. Missing INCLUDE classes are a dependency to source or collect, not proof that the concepts are absent from ISL or all available datasets. Product requirements should determine needed concepts; a convenient checkpoint should not determine the user's conversation.
- Exact signs/phrases for WAIT, REPEAT, NO/YES, cancellation and corrected locations still need appropriate examples and fluent review. The old 25-label list does not cover this by assertion.
- No dataset or hearing reenactment validates Deaf interaction, natural ISL, regional acceptance or reading preferences. If participant recruitment fails, honestly report technical proof and expert linguistic review separately from usability validation.

## 3. What evidence should the team prioritize, and what must the demo prove?

### Takeaway

Spend the first authorized engineering effort on one thin vertical slice, choose one task/channel, then make the unexpected turn and repair work. An impact narrative is credible when judges see user control and task completion; model count, perfect prerecorded clips and unsupported population statistics do not establish it.

### Cited Findings

- The event asks builders to create a real voice product and explains a later interview/finale selection sequence. That supports prioritizing a functioning submission and evidence, without claiming a particular judge preference. [Official page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)
- Participant evidence identifies dependence on hearing intermediaries and service-provider telephony as actual reported problems, alongside agency and privacy concerns. This makes independent coordination and explicit consent a defensible product goal. [Primary Indian DHH study](https://roshni-poddar.github.io/assets/pdf/interact23a-sub1087-cam-i7.pdf)
- Bolna event ordering/queue behavior and configurable silence repetition mean an event acknowledgment or short cached utterance alone cannot establish responsive sign-mediated negotiation. [Event injection](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/graph-agent/event-injection.md); [silence repeat](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/graph-agent/static-nodes.md)

### Inferences

**Prioritized evidence plan — proposed, no execution is authorized by these notes:**

1. **Choose one task and one channel by a real vertical slice.** Fixed committed text must pass through Bolna to actual audible output, and the hearing person's reply must reach the human signer in the chosen form. For native Phone this is system captions, not app ASR. For app-owned/Bolna calling this must be a verified actual media/transcript feed. A one- or two-day implementation gate is reasonable given the submission window; do not spend a week polishing UI around an unresolved transport.
2. **Prove the minimum recognition set and repair before adding breadth.** Inventory exact phrase/intent semantics, source examples and rights. Validate a different session and, if possible, a competent signer not in calibration. If the core negative/wait/correction cannot work, the conversation is not ready regardless of greeting accuracy.
3. **Run the complete counterfactual demonstration repeatedly.** The human counterpart chooses one of several alternatives at runtime; the signer can reject, wait, correct and approve. Show actual input/output and remaining limitations. Freeze the final supported scope before recording the submission.
4. **Spend remaining time on evidence and usability clarity.** Measure finish-of-sign to first audible output including segmentation/commitment/network/voice; false spoken utterances; duplicated/stale output; repair attempts; and task success. State denominators and device/language/session limits. Ask for Deaf/expert feedback through permitted outreach, but do not fabricate recruitment or send messages without authorization.

**Proposed submission demonstration:** ordinary pickup or administrative appointment request → counterpart supplies a deliberately different location/time → user reads actual reply → user signs refusal/correction → a natural signing pause leaves the system quiet or uses a deliberate approved hold → counterpart confirms the corrected detail → user explicitly approves/ends. Include one unsupported or badly framed input causing visible abstention rather than a confident guessed utterance. Keep details dynamic enough that changing a profile card or alternate offer demonstrably changes what is said.

**Why this is stronger than generic sign-to-speech:** Bolna mediates turn timing and the hearing side of an actual task; the user retains semantic authority; the hearing person keeps an ordinary interaction; the prototype demonstrates a repairable two-way exchange rather than one-way classification. Where Bolna is strictly an exact-voice pipeline in the native route, show its useful technical contribution explicitly and do not claim it hears the remote party. Where Bolna is an agent, disclose its delegated role instead of presenting its own decisions as the user's literal signing.

**Stop adding scope when:** another feature does not change task success, error recovery or access burden. Cut continuous unrestricted translation, large vocabulary, avatars, emergency handling, autonomous booking, extra decision-model layers and multi-mode polish before cutting honest uncertainty or correction. These are proposed product priorities, not externally mandated exclusions.

### Gaps

- Winning cannot be inferred from documentation; competitors, scoring, execution and judge preferences remain unknown. “Competitive direction” is an engineering/product judgment, not a promise.
- Timing thresholds and acceptance counts should be set before runtime testing; this note does not invent clinical reliability targets or claim statistical validation from a small demo.
- Final architecture needs the actual Bolna transport audit, runtime result, exact supported dialogue and source-clip audit. No implementation, calls, paid inference, downloads, installation or account mutation occurred for this note.
