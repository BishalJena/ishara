# Ishara launch film: script

**Length:** about 2 min 45 s · **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps · **Tone:** calm, exact, human.
No hype words, no "revolutionary". It's a film about independence, and it should feel that serious.

**How it's made:** motion graphics for the story and the numbers; **the real Ishara app** (its
SwiftUI screens, recorded from the iOS simulator) for the product, inside an iPhone 17 frame.
The call in the film is a scripted run of the app's own call screen (the receptionist's lines
are pre-written), with phone audio added in the edit. Production notes: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).

Positioning and numbers follow [PITCH.md](../../../PITCH.md) and [MARKET.md](../../../MARKET.md).
Never add the population figures together, and never call Ishara the first accessible calling app.

## 6. How it works (2:20–2:34)

One clean diagram builds left to right, each block lighting up as the signal passes through.
Logos only where we use the product; names in text otherwise.

```
 iPhone (SwiftUI)                         Ishara backend                       Bolna voice agent             The clinic
 ├ captions, steer, Live Activity   ⇄    FastAPI on Heroku               ⇄    Deepgram  speech → text   ⇄   their own phone
 ├ signs: MediaPipe + ASL model           Groq · gpt-oss-120b phrases           Sarvam    text → voice
 │  (on-device, LiteRT)                   a fact-checker blocks anything        Plivo     the phone line
 └ documents: Vision + PDFKit             you didn't say
   (read on the phone)
```

| On screen, in order | Line |
|---|---|
| iPhone | **Signs are recognised on the phone** (MediaPipe + the Kaggle ASL Signs model, 250 signs) |
| Backend | **An LLM phrases; a fact-checker rejects any day, time, number or name you didn't give** |
| Bolna | **Bolna runs the call: Deepgram hears, Sarvam speaks, Plivo connects** |
| Clinic | **The clinic just answers its phone. Nothing to install.** |
| Footer, small | Calls come from Ishara's calling number (Bolna), not yours · Signs: ASL today, ISL next |

**VO:** "Under the hood: on-device sign recognition, a language model that can't invent facts,
and Bolna's voice agent, with Deepgram listening and Sarvam speaking."

## Sources (checked 30 Oct 2026)

1. WHO, *Deafness and hearing loss* fact sheet: 430 million require rehabilitation for disabling
   hearing loss. https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/deafness-and-hearing-loss

   Deaf: more than 70 million Deaf people. https://www.un.org/en/observances/sign-languages-day
3. ISLRTC, *About ISLRTC*: about 50 lakh Deaf persons (Census 2011); fewer than 300 certified
   interpreters. https://islrtc.nic.in/about-department/about-islrtc/ · Census 2011 figure
   5,071,007 via PIB: https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=124291
4. FCC, *2025–26 TRS Fund contribution factors*: net funding requirement $1,479,568,862.

5. FCC, DA 24-620: VRS compensation $4.07–$8.06 per minute (2024–25).
   https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-24-620A1.txt

7. Ishara, MARKET.md: the $5/month, 5%-eligible scenario is a planning assumption, not a forecast.

## Rules this film keeps

- The receptionist, clinic, patient and numbers are fictional. No real patient data.
- The call shows what the app does today: Ishara discloses itself, retells only the typed request,
  shares document facts only when asked, previews the user's line, and closes on a confirmation.
- Signing is shown as ASL, recognised on the phone; the film doesn't claim ISL support.
- No claim of Deaf-user validation, adoption or partnerships.
